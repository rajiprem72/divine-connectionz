What is Raja Yoga Meditation?

Raja Yoga Meditation is a spiritual practice centred on the mind, awareness, self-realization and a conscious connection with the Divine. The teachings presented on Divine Connectionz encourage learners to understand themselves as souls, cultivate positive qualities and develop a peaceful and stable mind.

This website presents Raja Yoga as a path of learning and personal practice. You can study the concepts, listen to classes, watch lessons and practise meditation gradually according to your own understanding.
